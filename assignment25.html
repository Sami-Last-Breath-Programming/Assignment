Design Choices

Colour: clean dark slate theme with an emerald green accent for buttons and interactive highlights to ensure high readability and contrast.

Typography: system font stack for fast loading; 1.6 line-height for readable paragraphs.

Layout: content capped at 800px so lines stay comfortable to read; white cards on a neutral light-grey background create depth.

Maintainability: colours live in CSS variables so the theme can change easily in one place.
