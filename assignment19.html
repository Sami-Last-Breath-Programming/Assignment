Why each tag matters

  • charset – prevents garbled characters such as ₹ or é.
  • viewport – makes pages responsive and usable on mobile devices.
  • description – controls the snippet preview shown in search engine results.
  • Open Graph – controls how cards and previews appear when links are shared on social media.
